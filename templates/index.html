Primary Lens: Customer Segmentation

Unsupervised Learning

The customer-centric foundation leverages RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) metrics to classify each unique shopper into distinct behavioral archetypes based on purchasing frequency, order magnitude, and dormancy.

  • RFM Feature Engineering: Derived transactional recency in days, total order cadence, and total customer gross spend.
  • Multi-Model Benchmarking: Comparative evaluation across K-Means, Agglomerative Hierarchical, and DBSCAN algorithms.
  • Actionable Personas: Delineates Regular Average Shoppers, High-Value VIPs, First-time Exploratory Buyers, and Dormant At-Risk Accounts.
  • Strategic Objective: Minimize churn, protect core cash-flow accounts, and optimize lifecycle communication cadences.

Secondary Lens: Product Association

Market Basket Analysis

The item-level perspective uncovers hidden co-occurrence patterns across shopping baskets through Apriori Association Rule Mining, generating high-lift product bundles tailored to each customer cluster.

  • Market Basket Mining: Evaluates transactional co-occurrences utilizing Support, Confidence, and Lift metrics.
  • Segment-Specific Affinity: Discovers which item pairs appeal strongest to distinct customer purchasing tiers.
  • High Lift Multipliers: Top recommendation bundles exhibit association lifts exceeding 55x to 64x over random chance.
  • Strategic Objective: Elevate Average Order Value (AOV), design complementary package promotions, and increase item attach rates.
